Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The Legendary Tutor ("we," "us," "our") connects learners with vetted tutors. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a Request a Tutor form, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, subject(s), academic level, examination, preferred lesson type, preferred days and time, location, budget, learning goals, and any additional notes you provide.

When you submit a Become a Tutor application, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, gender, location, subjects and academic levels taught, years of experience, qualifications, your CV, any certificates you upload, teaching mode, availability, expected pay, and your personal statement.

When you use our Contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject line, and message.

We do not use cookies or tracking analytics on this website at this time.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to review learning requests and tutor applications, personally match learners with a suitable tutor from our vetted network, communicate with you about your request, application, or enquiry (by email or WhatsApp), and maintain the records needed to operate our matching service.

3. How Submissions Reach Us

Form submissions on this website are delivered to our team by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. FormSubmit receives what you submit only to forward it to us. We do not currently store submissions in a database; as our platform grows, we may introduce secure database storage for matching and record-keeping, and this policy will be updated to reflect that.

4. Sharing Your Information

We share a learner's relevant details with the tutor they are matched with, and a tutor's relevant details with matched learners, so that lessons can begin. We also share information with service providers who help us operate the site (such as FormSubmit), solely to deliver that service.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Learners Under 18

Many of the students we support are preparing for secondary school examinations and may be under 18. Where a learner is under 18, we ask that a parent or guardian submit the Request a Tutor form, or review and approve it, on the learner's behalf. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us without appropriate parental or guardian involvement, please contact us and we will address it.

6. Data Retention

We keep the information you submit for as long as needed to complete a match, provide our service, and maintain reasonable business records, after which it is deleted or anonymized unless you ask us to retain it for a specific ongoing purpose.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us, including relying on encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website and limiting who on our team can view submissions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion, subject to any records we are required to keep for legitimate business or legal reasons.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to adinoyi4all@gmail.com, or via WhatsApp at 0802 586 3243 or 0706 493 7769.

This policy describes our actual data practices in plain language and is not a substitute for legal advice about your specific circumstances.
