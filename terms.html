Our Role

The Legendary Tutor is a matching service. We connect learners with independent tutors from our vetted network based on the requirements submitted through our Request a Tutor form. We do not guarantee specific academic outcomes.

Learners agree to provide accurate information when requesting a tutor, to communicate respectfully with their matched tutor, and to make agreed payments for lessons as arranged.

Tutor Responsibilities

Tutors agree to provide accurate information about their qualifications and experience, to deliver lessons professionally and reliably, and to maintain appropriate conduct with learners at all times.
